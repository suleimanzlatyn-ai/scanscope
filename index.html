About Scan Scope Technologies
Inspection you can build decisions on.

Scan Scope Technologies provides professional non-destructive testing and inspection services designed to identify defects without unnecessarily damaging the asset being inspected. Our approach combines inspection methods, modern equipment and clear reporting to support maintenance and engineering decisions.

  • Non-destructive examination of components and structures.
  • Professional inspection reporting.
  • Technology selected for practical field inspection.

Our Services
Advanced NDT solutions.

Our services use non-destructive inspection methods to help locate defects, discontinuities and material changes while preserving the asset being examined.

  • Ultrasonic Testing: examination for internal discontinuities and material thickness.
  • NDT Radiography: radiographic examination of welds, castings and components.
  • Visual Inspection: detailed visual examination using professional inspection equipment and techniques.
