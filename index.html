Frequently asked questions

Before you request an inspection.

What information should I send first?

Share the asset or component type, material if known, site location, inspection objective, applicable specification and desired timing. Photos or drawings may help clarify the scope.

Can you recommend an inspection method?

We can discuss the information needed to assess method suitability. The appropriate method depends on the asset, material, access, suspected issue and project requirements.

Do you work to a specific code or standard?

Tell us the governing code, specification or client procedure. Applicability, personnel requirements and deliverables must be confirmed for the project before work is agreed.

How do I get a quotation?

Send the asset details, location, inspection objective and schedule through the enquiry form or WhatsApp. We can then clarify any missing information needed to define the scope.
