FAQ

Questions clients often have.

What is NDT?
Non-destructive testing examines a material, component or structure without intentionally damaging the asset being inspected.
Which inspection method should I request?
It depends on the asset, material, access and inspection objective. Tell us what you need inspected and we can discuss the appropriate approach.
Do you support pipelines and industrial assets?
Yes. The site presents pipeline, petrochemical and refining, infrastructure, construction and fabrication inspection support among the areas served.
Can I request an inspection by phone or WhatsApp?
Yes. Call or WhatsApp 0721 449 727 with the basic project details and your inspection requirement.
What information should I provide when contacting you?
Useful details include the asset type, location, inspection method if known, quantity or scope, access conditions and preferred timing.
Do you work only on one type of industry?
No. The current service positioning covers pipelines, petrochemical and refining, construction and fabrication, infrastructure and other industrial inspection needs.
