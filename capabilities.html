Choosing the right method

Capabilities should match the inspection need

Different methods reveal different types of indications. The final method and acceptance criteria should be agreed with the client and relevant technical personnel.

01 / UT

Internal examination

Can be used for suitable materials to evaluate internal discontinuities or measure wall thickness.

02 / RT

Radiographic imaging

Can reveal volumetric features in suitable components; access, geometry and radiation controls are key considerations.

03 / VT

Surface condition

Provides direct visual assessment of accessible areas, subject to visibility, access and defined criteria.

04 / Other methods

Scope-specific options

Ask about other methods such as magnetic particle or liquid penetrant testing; availability and suitability must be confirmed.

Important: Listing a method does not mean every project is suitable for it. Method availability, personnel qualifications, equipment and required approvals should be confirmed before a commitment is made.

FAQs

Questions before requesting an inspection

If you are unsure which service you need, send us the details you have and ask for a scope discussion.

What information should I provide for a quotation?

Share your company and contact details, site location, asset or weld type, quantity, required timeline, relevant drawings or specifications, and any known inspection method.

Can you recommend an inspection method?

The appropriate method depends on material, geometry, likely defect type, access, acceptance criteria and the applicable standard. The requirement should be reviewed by a suitably qualified technical person.

Do you provide inspection reports?

Reporting format and contents should be agreed as part of the quotation, including the scope, results and any required supporting records.

Do you work outside Nairobi?

Contact us with the project location and schedule. Coverage, travel, availability and any mobilization costs must be confirmed for each enquiry.

Can you support urgent projects?

Send the deadline and site requirements. We will need to confirm personnel, equipment, access and scheduling before accepting an urgent job.

Are all listed inspection methods always available?

No. Method availability, qualified personnel, equipment, safety requirements and project suitability must be confirmed before the service is booked.

Have an inspection requirement?

Tell us what needs to be inspected and where the work is located. We can discuss the next steps and confirm the information needed for a quotation.
