ABOUT SCAN SCOPE TECHNOLOGIES

Inspection expertise. Clear communication. Practical decisions.

We help clients plan inspection work around the asset, operating environment and information they need to make informed decisions.

Who we are

Built around the needs of the asset and the people responsible for it.

ScanScope Technologies supports non-destructive testing and inspection enquiries for industrial, infrastructure and construction-related assets. Our approach begins with understanding the component, its condition, the access available and the decision the inspection must support.

Rather than assuming one method suits every situation, we work with clients to clarify the inspection scope, project requirements, site conditions and reporting expectations before work is agreed.

How we work

Clear steps. No guesswork.

Every enquiry is different. We use a structured process to establish what is needed before confirming scope and delivery.

01

Understand the requirement

Discuss the asset, inspection objective, location, access, operating conditions and timing.

02

Agree the inspection scope

Review suitable methods, applicable project requirements, limitations and deliverables before confirming the work.

03

Communicate the findings

Set clear expectations for reporting, findings, limitations and any follow-up actions within the agreed scope.

Our principles

Professional practice starts with being clear.

  • Scope and objectives agreed before work begins
  • Method selection considered against the asset and requirement
  • Safety, access and operating constraints discussed early
  • Transparent communication about limitations and deliverables
  • Project-specific standards and qualifications verified before commitment
