What a useful case study should show

01

The asset and objective

02

03

Reporting and next steps

RESPONSIBLE DISCLOSURE

Project confidentiality matters

We will not invent completed projects, client names, testimonials or performance figures. Client-specific photographs and reports should be published only when permission has been granted. If you are evaluating ScanScope for a tender, contact the team to discuss relevant experience and available supporting information.

Inspection scenarios

These are example situations to help explain the kinds of questions inspection can address. They are not claims of completed ScanScope projects.

01

Weld inspection planning

A project team needs to confirm weld inspection requirements, applicable acceptance criteria and reporting expectations before work begins.

02

Corrosion or wall-thickness concern

An asset owner needs to understand whether thickness measurements are suitable for the location, access conditions and decision being made.

03

Existing structure assessment

A contractor needs to establish the inspection objective and identify a suitable approach for the structure and available access.
