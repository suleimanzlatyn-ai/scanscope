WHO WE ARE

Revolutionizing non-destructive testing.

In a field where precision and reliability are everything, Scan Scope Technologies has built a reputation as a forward-looking NDT partner. We design and apply advanced inspection methods across a wide range of materials, structures and assets — always without cutting, dismantling or damaging what we're testing.

Every engagement starts with the client's real operating conditions. Rather than applying one method everywhere, our engineers select and combine techniques so that inspection findings are accurate, repeatable and directly useful for the decisions our clients need to make.

OUR SERVICES

Your trusted NDT partner.

From aircraft components to city water mains, we tailor inspection programs to the material, the environment and the risk you're managing.

Aerospace crack detection

Reliable identification of surface and sub-surface cracks in aircraft components, using methods matched to the demands of aviation safety standards.

Pipeline leakage detection

Guided wave, ultrasonic and acoustic emission testing to catch leaks early across oil, gas, water and chemical pipeline networks.

Corrosion in water pipes

Years of contact between water and metal takes a toll. We assess corrosion and wall-thickness loss without opening up the line.

Explosives detection

Industrial NDT imaging, neutron radiography and millimeter-wave scanning to non-intrusively screen objects and structures for concealed threats.

Building crack detection

Ultrasonic testing and industrial NDT imaging examine structural elements from the inside out, flagging voids and defects before they become hazards.

Hazardous site chemical cleaning

Careful inspection of equipment and structures in corrosive or toxic environments, paired with ultrasonic and magnetic particle testing.

OUR EQUIPMENT

Field-ready inspection technology.

We deploy purpose-built instruments for each method, kept calibrated and field-tested so every reading holds up under review.

CRAWLER SYSTEM

Tripod-mounted inspection crawler

A tripod-stabilized crawler with handheld controller for guided visual and remote inspection inside pipes, vessels and hard-to-reach structures.

UT GAUGE

Digital ultrasonic thickness gauge

Handheld gauge with a probe on a coiled lead for fast, precise wall-thickness and corrosion readings on pipework, tanks and structural plate.

RADIOGRAPHY

Portable X-ray radiography unit

Pistol-grip X-ray source with tripod support for imaging welds, castings and internal structures without cutting into the asset.

AE MONITORING

Acoustic emission sensor array

Magnetic-mount sensors wired to a central logger, listening for stress waves in real time to flag crack growth and active leaks.

MPI

Magnetic particle inspection yoke

Handheld electromagnetic yoke with power pack, used for surface and near-surface crack detection on ferromagnetic materials.

VIDEO SCOPE

Flexible video borescope

Handheld display console with an articulating insertion tube for visual inspection deep inside engines, tanks and enclosed cavities.
