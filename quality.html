QUALITY • SAFETY • DOCUMENTATION

Responsible inspection starts before the site visit.

Inspection quality depends on suitable planning, capable personnel, appropriate equipment, agreed acceptance criteria and clear reporting.

A disciplined approach to inspection delivery.

Requirements vary by asset, industry and contract. We confirm the applicable controls, qualifications, equipment and documentation for each engagement rather than making blanket claims.

✓

Scope review

Clarify objectives, component details, access, inspection coverage and acceptance criteria before work is confirmed.

✓

Method suitability

Consider the material, geometry, access and expected discontinuities alongside project specifications and method limitations.

✓

Documentation

Agree the records, reports, traceability and deliverables required under the project scope.

✓

Personnel & equipment

Confirm the qualifications, authorisations, equipment and calibration evidence required for the specific work.

✓

Site safety

Discuss site induction, permits, risk assessments, PPE, isolation and access restrictions as applicable.

✓

Transparent limitations

Communicate practical constraints and any limitations that affect interpretation or inspection coverage.

Standards & compliance

Project requirements come first.

Applicable codes, standards, acceptance criteria and client procedures should be identified and agreed before work starts.

  • Confirm the governing code or specification
  • Identify acceptance criteria and reporting expectations
  • Verify personnel certification requirements for the scope
  • Confirm equipment readiness and calibration requirements
  • Record limitations, exclusions and agreed deviations

What we confirm for each project

Scope and method · site conditions · required qualifications · equipment requirements · safety controls · report format · schedule and access.

We do not claim a particular certification, accreditation or standard compliance unless it is verified and applicable to the engagement.
