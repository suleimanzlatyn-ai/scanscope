INSPECTION METHOD

Thickness Measurement

Thickness measurement can help evaluate remaining wall thickness on suitable components and support corrosion monitoring. Interpretation depends on the measurement plan, material, surface and reference requirements.

SCANSCOPE TECHNOLOGIES

Common use cases

01

Pipes and process lines

Measure agreed points on accessible pipework, subject to material and access suitability.

02

Tanks and vessels

A defined measurement grid may establish thickness at selected locations with coverage agreed for the project.

03

Repeat monitoring

Consistent location identification and records can support comparison over time when procedures and conditions are comparable.

SCANSCOPE TECHNOLOGIES

What to specify

  • Asset type, material and nominal thickness
  • Number and location of measurement points
  • Surface coating, access and operating conditions
  • Previous readings or drawings, if available
  • Required units, reporting format and decision criteria
SCANSCOPE TECHNOLOGIES

Important limitation

A thickness reading alone is not a remaining-life calculation or fitness-for-service assessment. Such conclusions require appropriate data, methods and competent engineering evaluation.
